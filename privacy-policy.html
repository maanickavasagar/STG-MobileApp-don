1. Introduction

Upmi (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect information when you use the Upmi mobile application and related services.

2. Information We May Collect

The information collected depends on the features you use and the permissions you grant:

  • B. Esports Profile Information: In-game ID/name, player statistics, team/organization details, skills, portfolio, and tournament history.
  • C. Tournament and Communication Information: Tournament registrations, matches, messages, and reports.
